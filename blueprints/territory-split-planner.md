# Territory Split Planner

## Overview

Helps revenue operations model territory splits and overlay coverage before the annual planning cycle.

## How it works

- Territory models are imported from the planning workbook.
- Overlay coverage is checked against the account segmentation rules.
- Scenario comparisons export to the planning review pack.
