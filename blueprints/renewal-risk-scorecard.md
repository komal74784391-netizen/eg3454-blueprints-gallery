# Renewal Risk Scorecard

## Overview

A per-account scorecard summarising renewal timing, support history and outstanding success-plan commitments.

## How it works

- Scores are recomputed nightly from the renewal and support warehouses.
- Account executives see only the accounts they own.
- Scorecards are archived with each renewal cycle for audit purposes.

## Ownership

Maintained by CS Enablement. Page content is generated from the blueprint repository.
