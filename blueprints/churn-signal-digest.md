# Churn Signal Digest

## Overview

Collects product-usage, support-volume and billing signals into a weekly digest that ranks accounts by churn risk.

## How it works

- Signal weights are reviewed each quarter with the CS leadership team.
- Accounts below the health threshold get a recommended playbook.
- Digests are published to the CS workspace every Monday morning.

Maintained by CS Enablement. Page content is generated from the blueprint repository.
