# Quote Approval Workflow

## Overview

Routes discount requests through the approval ladder based on deal size, margin and the requesting sales team.

## How it works

- Approval ladders are derived from the current discount policy matrix.
- Every decision records the approver, the margin impact and the SLA clock.
- Approved quotes are handed back to the CPQ connector automatically.
