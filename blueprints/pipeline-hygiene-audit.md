# Pipeline Hygiene Audit

## Overview

Checks the open pipeline for stale stages, missing next steps and duplicate opportunities before the weekly forecast call.

## How it works

- Rules mirror the forecast review checklist owned by Revenue Systems.
- Findings are grouped by owning team and delivered as a weekly digest.
- Historical findings are retained for trend reporting.

## Ownership

Maintained by Revenue Systems. Page content is generated from the blueprint repository.
